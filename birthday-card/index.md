# Pop-Up Birthday Card

A birthday card for iPhone Duo that they open by unfolding the phone. Hand it over folded and the card is on the cover. As the phone opens, a three-tier cake stands up, five candles light one after another, and confetti bursts from the corners. They tap the cake to make a wish. You can have it as layered paper-cut art or as a photograph of a picnic in the park at golden hour, and it works on any iPhone.
