# Pop-Up Birthday Card: Privacy Policy

_Effective November 10, 2026_

Pop-Up Birthday Card is made by Gary Guo. This page explains how the app handles your information. In short: **it stays on your iPhone.**

## Information we collect

**None.** The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs, and it never goes online. It does not collect personal information or usage data, does not send anything to a server, and does not track you across other apps or websites. Nothing is shared or sold.

## What the app keeps on your iPhone

The card works without any setup. If you personalise it in the hidden setup, the app saves those choices on your iPhone so the card remembers them: the birthday person's name, your message, your name, the tune and the style (Illustrated or Photograph). They are only copied anywhere else as part of your own device backups. Deleting the app removes them.

On a foldable iPhone, the app uses the hinge position only to open and close the card. It does not store or send it.

## Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you tap Record in the hidden setup to leave a voice message. The recording is saved on your iPhone and plays when the card opens. It is never uploaded or shared. You can delete it from the setup screen, and deleting the app deletes it too. If you never record a message, the app never asks for the microphone.

## Purchases

You pay for the app once, through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, so your payment details and Apple Account are never shared with us.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
