# Pop-Up Birthday Card: Support

Something not working, or an idea for the card? Write to **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll reply.

## How does the card open?

On iPhone Duo, close the phone and the front of the card shows on the cover display. As you unfold it, the pop-up stands up with the hinge and the candles light. Fold it and unfold it again to see the opening again. On other iPhones, tap the front to open the card, then tap the small envelope in the bottom corner to close it.

## How do they make a wish?

In the Illustrated style, tap the cake once the card is fully open. All five candles go out, and a few seconds later they relight for the next wish. In the Photograph style, tap the candles one by one, or swipe quickly across the flames. Tap a candle that's out to light it again. If all of them are out, they relight by themselves after a few seconds.

## How do I add their name, my own message or a voice message?

You don't have to: the card is complete as it is. To personalise it, press and hold the **top-left corner** of the card for about a second, or tap three times quickly on the open card. This opens the hidden setup. There you can add the birthday person's name, write your own message, add your name under "Love always", pick one of three tunes, pick the style and record a voice message of up to 30 seconds. Tap **Done** to return to the card.

## How do I switch between Illustrated and Photograph?

Open the hidden setup (see above) and choose a tile under **Style**.

## How do I put back the card's original words?

In the hidden setup, tap **Restore the card's own words**. This clears the name and your name and brings back the original message.

## There's no music

Turn the volume up. The card plays its music even when the phone is set to silent. The music begins once the card is open.

## Why would the app want the microphone?

Only to record your voice message, and only after you tap Record in the hidden setup. The recording stays on your iPhone. If you never record anything, the app never asks.

## Does it need Wi-Fi or data?

No. Everything the card needs is inside the app, so it works offline.

## My screen doesn't lock while the card is open

That's intentional: the card stays on screen while you pass the phone over. Your usual auto-lock comes back as soon as you leave the app.
