# Foldfly: Privacy Policy

_Effective October 29, 2026_

Foldfly is a game made by Gary Guo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Foldfly does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your device so the game works the way you left it: your best scores, your settings (control mode, tap setting, phone colour, map and avatar), today's free trial runs, and whether you own Foldfly Plus. These never leave your device, except as part of your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

The app reads your phone's hinge angle (on foldable phones) only to play the game, in real time. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Foldfly Plus is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details or your account. The app only receives a confirmation from the App Store that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock the content. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

## Children

Foldfly is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
