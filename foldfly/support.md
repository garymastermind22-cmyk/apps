# Foldfly: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll get back to you.

## How do I play?

- **Foldable phone:** hold the phone part-open like a book and give the hinge a quick, small squeeze to flap. You don't need to close it all the way.
- **Bend mode:** the hinge angle becomes your altitude. Open to climb, fold to dive.
- **Any phone:** tap anywhere to flap. Every map and feature works by tapping.

## My flaps aren't registering on the hinge

Small, quick squeezes work best. Let the hinge settle for a moment after each squeeze. You can switch to tap controls at any time in Settings.

## How do I restore Foldfly Plus?

Tap **Plus** on the title screen, then **Restore Purchases**. It's also in **Settings > Purchases**. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

## What does Foldfly Plus include?

Foldfly Plus is a one-time purchase that unlocks three premium maps and four extra avatars. Each premium map also gives you one free trial run per day without buying.

## Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your phone. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
