# Pop-Up Christmas Card: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll get back to you.

## How do I open the card?

On iPhone Duo, fold the phone shut: the front of the card waits on the cover display. Unfold it and the card opens: the seal breaks, the front swings open and the pop-up rises. Fold it and unfold it again to replay the opening. On any other iPhone, tap the card to open it, and tap the small envelope in the bottom corner to close it again.

## How do I add a name, my own message or a voice message?

The card works for anyone as it is. To personalise it, press and hold the **top-left corner** of the card for a second, or triple-tap the open card. That opens the hidden setup, where you can add their name, your message and your name, choose the carol, choose the style (Illustrated or Photograph) and record a voice message of up to 30 seconds. Tap **Done** to go back to the card.

## How do I switch between Illustrated and Photograph?

Open the hidden setup (see above) and pick a tile under **Style**.

## I don't hear any music

Turn the volume up. The card plays its music even when the phone is set to silent. The carol starts once the card is fully open.

## Why does the app ask for the microphone?

Only if you tap Record in the hidden setup, to record your voice message. The recording stays on your iPhone. If you never record one, the app never asks.

## Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is in the app and it works fully offline.

## The screen doesn't turn off while the card is showing

That's on purpose, so the card is still showing when you hand the phone over. When you leave the app, your normal auto-lock comes back.
