# Pop-Up Christmas Card

A real Christmas card that lives on your phone, made for iPhone Duo. Hand it over closed: the card waits on the cover. Open it, and the wax seal breaks and a winter village pops up with a carol and a handwritten message. It also works on any iPhone.
