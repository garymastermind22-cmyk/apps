The hinge test

  • Open the Test tab and tap Start the test. It takes about a minute.
  • 1. Close it fully. 2. Open and close slowly all the way flat and back, three times. 3. Hold at 90° (an L shape) as still as you can for 5 seconds. 4. Table check: close it, lay it on a table, and open it slowly until it lies flat, keeping the bottom half on the table. If asked, flip the phone over and do the last step again.
  • You get a 0–100 score and a band (A Excellent, B Good, C Fair, D Rough). Slow, smooth movements give the most accurate result. Take off a thick case if it stops the phone closing or opening fully.

What the score means (and doesn't)

The score describes how the hinge and its angle sensor behaved during this test: whether it reads fully shut and fully flat, how smoothly the reading moves, how steady it is at 90°, how often it updates, whether every sweep lands in the same place, and whether it agrees with the motion sensor. It can't see wear or past damage, and it isn't a medical, repair or warranty check. If your score stays low after a few slow retests, have the phone checked by an authorised service provider.

Fold counting

iOS shares the hinge angle with an app only while it's open on screen. FoldCheck counts an opening each time it sees the phone go from shut (under 8°) to open (30° or more), and adds up the time in each posture while it's open. It can't count folds from before you installed it, or while it's closed or in the background.
