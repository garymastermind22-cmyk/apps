FoldCheck: Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

FoldCheck is an app made by Gary Guo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. FoldCheck does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party code, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites. It does not use the network at all, apart from the App Store's own purchase system.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your device so it can show your history:

  • the folds it counted and the time spent in each posture and angle, per day, while FoldCheck was open;
  • your hinge test results (score, each metric, date);
  • a few settings (onboarding seen, whether you own FoldCheck Pro).

These never leave your device, except as part of your own device backups or a CSV export you choose to share yourself. Deleting the app deletes them, and Settings in the app can delete them at any time.

Sensors

FoldCheck reads the hinge angle while it's open. During the table step of the hinge test it also reads the phone's motion sensor (gravity) to work out the opening angle a second way. Readings are used on the phone only; the app never asks for or uses your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or health data.

Purchases

FoldCheck Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details or your account. The app only receives a confirmation from the App Store that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock the details. Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

Children

FoldCheck is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
