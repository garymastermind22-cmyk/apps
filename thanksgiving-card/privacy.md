# Pop-Up Thanksgiving Card: Privacy Policy

_Effective November 10, 2026_

Pop-Up Thanksgiving Card is an app made by Gary Guo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It never connects to the internet. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

## What stays on your phone

If you personalise the card in its hidden setup, the app saves your choices on your device: the recipient's name, your message, your name, the music and the style. They never leave your device, except as part of your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge position only to open and close the card. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

## Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only if you choose to record a voice message in the hidden setup. The recording is saved on your device, plays when the card is opened, and is never uploaded or shared. You can delete it in setup, and deleting the app deletes it. If you never record a message, the app never asks.

## Purchases

The app is a paid download. Apple handles the payment entirely through the App Store, so we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
