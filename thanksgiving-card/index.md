# Pop-Up Thanksgiving Card

A real Thanksgiving card that lives on your phone, made for iPhone Duo. Hand it over closed: the card waits on the cover. Open it, and a harvest table pops up under a big old oak at golden hour, with a gentle hymn and a handwritten message. It also works on any iPhone.
