# Fairway Duo Golf: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll get back to you.

## How do I play?

Aim, pick a club and swing. The whole hole is in view, so you can plan around bunkers, water and the wind. On a foldable phone, unfold for the wide view of the course; folded, the outer screen plays the same game.

## How do I restore a purchase?

If you bought the Plus unlock, use **Restore Purchases** in the app with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

## Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your phone. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
