# Fairway Duo Golf: Privacy Policy

_Effective October 30, 2026_

Fairway Duo Golf is a game made by Gary Guo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Fairway Duo Golf does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your device so the game works the way you left it: your settings (units, handedness, sound, haptics, aim assist), your best scores and shots, the routes and coins you've found, and any purchase you've made. These never leave your device, except as part of your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

On foldable phones the app reads the hinge angle only to lay out the game, in real time. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Any in-app purchase is optional and handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details or your account. The app only receives a confirmation from the App Store that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock the content. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

## Children

Fairway Duo Golf is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
