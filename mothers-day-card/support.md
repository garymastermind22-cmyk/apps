# Pop-Up Mother's Day Card: Support

Found a bug, need a hand, or have an idea? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll reply.

## How does the card open?

On iPhone Duo, fold the phone shut and the front of the card waits on the cover display. Unfold it and the garden stands up as the hinge opens. Fold and unfold again to replay the whole opening. On any other iPhone, tap the front: the wax seal cracks and the card swings open. Tap the small envelope in the corner to close it again.

## Can I give it to my partner rather than my mom?

Yes. The card's own message suits either. In the hidden setup (below) there are also one-tap messages written "For Mom" and "For my partner", or you can write your own.

## How do I add her name, my own words or a voice message?

Press and hold the **top-left corner** of the card for about a second, or triple-tap the open card. The hidden setup opens. There you can add her name, choose or write the message, add your name, pick the music and the style (Illustrated or Photograph), and record a voice message of up to 30 seconds. Tap **Done** to go back to the card. "Restore the card's own words" puts the original message back.

## How do I switch between Illustrated and Photograph?

Open the hidden setup and pick a tile under **Style**.

## I can't hear the music

Turn the volume up. The music plays even with the phone on silent, and it starts once the card is fully open.

## Why does the app want the microphone?

Only when you tap Record in the hidden setup, to record your voice message. The recording stays on your iPhone. If you never record, the app never asks.

## Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the app, and it works fully offline.

## Why doesn't the screen turn off?

On purpose: the card should still be showing when you hand her the phone. Your normal auto-lock returns as soon as you leave the app.
