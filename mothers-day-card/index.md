# Pop-Up Mother's Day Card

A Mother's Day card she opens with her own hands, made for iPhone Duo. Give her the phone folded shut with the card on the cover. As she unfolds it, a paper garden grows up from the page: buds open into peonies, tulips and roses, butterflies lift off, a watering can sprinkles the flowers, and your message writes itself by hand to a gentle melody. Choose an illustrated paper-cut garden or a photograph of a château garden in spring. It also works on any iPhone.
