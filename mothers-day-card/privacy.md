# Pop-Up Mother's Day Card: Privacy Policy

_Effective November 10, 2026_

Pop-Up Mother's Day Card is an app by Gary Guo. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: **it all stays on your phone.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app collects no personal information or usage data, and it never shares or sells any. It makes no internet connections. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

## What the app keeps on your phone

If you personalise the card in its hidden setup, your choices are saved on your device only: her name, the message you picked or wrote, your name, the music and the style. They leave your device only as part of your own device backups, and deleting the app removes them.

On foldable iPhones the app reads the hinge position to open and close the card. That reading is never stored or sent anywhere.

## Microphone

The microphone is used only if you choose to record a voice message in the hidden setup. The recording is saved on your device, plays when the card opens, and is never uploaded or shared. You can delete it in setup at any time, and deleting the app deletes it too. If you never record, the app never asks for the microphone.

## Purchases

The app is a paid download. Apple handles the payment through the App Store; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.
