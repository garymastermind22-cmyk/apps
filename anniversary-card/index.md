# Pop-Up Anniversary Card

An anniversary card made for iPhone Duo, given folded shut. "Happy Anniversary" waits on the cover beside a wax seal of two gold rings. Unfold the phone and a moonlit garden grows with the hinge around an ivory gazebo, two paper lanterns rise together, and a ribbon ties itself into a bow, with Pachelbel, Satie or Bach and a handwritten message. Prefer a photograph? Choose Venice at blue hour instead. It also works on any iPhone.
