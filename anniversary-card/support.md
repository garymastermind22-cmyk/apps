# Pop-Up Anniversary Card: Support

A question, a bug or an idea? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll reply.

## How do I open the card?

On iPhone Duo, fold the phone shut and the front of the card appears on the cover display. Unfold it and the garden grows with the hinge; once it's fully open, the ribbon ties its bow. Fold and unfold again to replay the opening. On any other iPhone, tap the front to open the card, and tap the small envelope in the bottom corner to close it.

## How do I add a name, our years together or a voice message?

The card works as it is. To personalise it, press and hold the **top-left corner** of the card for about a second, or triple-tap the open card. The hidden setup lets you add their name, your message and your name, enter your years together (1 to 99), choose the music and the style, and record a voice message of up to 30 seconds. Tap **Done** to return to the card.

## Where do the years together appear?

In the Illustrated style, the number glows softly inside the gazebo a moment after the bow is tied. The Photograph style doesn't show it. Leave the field empty to hide it.

## How do I switch between Illustrated and Photograph?

Open the hidden setup (see above) and choose a tile under **Style**.

## I don't hear any music

Turn the volume up. The card plays its music even when the phone is set to silent. The music begins once the card is fully open.

## Why does the app ask for the microphone?

Only if you tap Record in the hidden setup, to record your voice message. The recording stays on your iPhone. If you never record one, the app never asks.

## Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the app, and it works fully offline.

## The screen stays on while the card is showing

That's deliberate, so the card is still there when you hand the phone over. As soon as you leave the app, your usual auto-lock returns.
