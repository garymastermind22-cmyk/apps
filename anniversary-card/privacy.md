# Pop-Up Anniversary Card: Privacy Policy

_Effective November 10, 2026_

Pop-Up Anniversary Card is an app made by Gary Guo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In one line: **everything stays on your phone.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal information or usage data, and it never connects to the internet. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

## What stays on your phone

If you personalise the card in its hidden setup, the app saves your choices on your device: the recipient's name, your message, your name, the number of years together, the music and the style. They stay on your device, apart from your own device backups, and deleting the app deletes them.

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge position only to open and close the card. It is never recorded or sent anywhere.

## Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only if you choose to record a voice message in the hidden setup. The recording is saved on your device, plays when the card opens, and is never uploaded or shared. You can remove it in setup, and deleting the app deletes it. If you never record a message, the app never asks.

## Purchases

The app is a paid download. Apple handles payment entirely through the App Store; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
