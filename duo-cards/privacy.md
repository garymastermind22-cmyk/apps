# Duo Cards: Privacy Policy

_Effective November 14, 2026_

Duo Cards (and its App Clip) is made by Gary Guo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **we collect nothing.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

## What stays on your phone

When you personalise a card, the app saves your choices on your device: the selected occasion, the recipient's name, your message, your name, the music and the style. They never leave your device, except as part of your own device backups or a card link you choose to share. Deleting the app deletes them.

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge position only to open and close the card. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

## Sharing a card

If you choose **Send Current Card**, the app creates a link containing the card's occasion, names, message, style and music, and hands it to the share method you pick. Anyone who receives the link can read that text and forward it. Opening the link shows the card in our App Clip, which reads the card from the link itself; we run no server and receive nothing. Voice recordings are never included in a link.

## Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only if you choose to record a voice message. The recording is saved on your device, plays when the card is opened, and is never uploaded or shared. You can delete it in setup, and deleting the app deletes it. If you never record a message, the app never asks.

## Purchases

The app is a paid download. Apple handles the payment entirely through the App Store, so we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
