# Duo Cards

Six living pop-up greeting cards in one app, made for iPhone Duo: Christmas, Birthday, Valentine's Day, Anniversary, Mother's Day and Thanksgiving. Hand over your phone folded shut, and as they unfold it the card opens with it: the scene rises, the music starts and your message writes itself in. It also works on any iPhone.
