# Duo Cards: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll get back to you.

## How do I open the card?

On iPhone Duo, fold the phone shut: the front of the card waits on the cover display. Unfold it and the card opens with the hinge. Fold it and unfold it again to replay the opening. On any other iPhone, tap the card to open it, and tap the small envelope in the bottom corner to close it again.

## How do I switch to another occasion?

Triple-tap the card (front or inside). The card picker shows all six occasions: Christmas, Birthday, Valentine's Day, Anniversary, Mother's Day and Thanksgiving. Pick one and tap **Done**. The app remembers your choice and opens straight to that card next time. You can also press and hold the top-left corner of the card for a second.

## How do I add a name, my own message or a voice message?

Triple-tap the card. Below the picker you can add their name, your message and your name, choose the music, choose the style (Illustrated or Photograph) and record a voice message of up to 30 seconds. Every occasion keeps its own words, music and recording.

## How do I send a card to someone?

Triple-tap the card and choose **Send Current Card**. Share the link however you like. The recipient opens the same card, with your names, message, style and music, in an App Clip on their iPhone. Your voice recording is never included in the link.

## I don't hear any music

Turn the volume up. The music starts once the card is fully open, even when the phone is set to silent.

## Why does the app ask for the microphone?

Only if you tap Record, to record your voice message. The recording stays on your iPhone. If you never record one, the app never asks.

## Does it need the internet?

No. Everything is in the app and it works fully offline.

## The screen doesn't turn off while the card is showing

That's on purpose, so the card is still showing when you hand the phone over. When you leave the app, your normal auto-lock comes back.
