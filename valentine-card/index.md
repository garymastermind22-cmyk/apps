# Pop-Up Valentine Card

A Valentine's card made for iPhone Duo that blooms as it opens. Hand over the phone folded shut, with "Happy Valentine's Day" waiting on the cover. As it unfolds, paper roses open one by one along a rose-gold garden arch, a heart-shaped locket swings open, and two doves meet, with a romantic classic playing and a handwritten message. Prefer a photograph? It also comes as a sunset over an Italian cliffside town. Works on any iPhone.
