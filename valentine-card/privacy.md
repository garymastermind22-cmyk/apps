# Pop-Up Valentine Card: Privacy Policy

_Effective November 10, 2026_

Pop-Up Valentine Card is made by Gary Guo. A Valentine is personal, so here is exactly what the app does with your information. In one line: **it all stays on your phone.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app makes no network connections at all. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. We never collect, store on a server, share or sell personal information or usage data.

## What stays on your phone

Anything you add in the card's hidden setup is saved only on your device: their name, your message, your name, the music you picked and the style. It never leaves your device, apart from your own device backups. Delete the app and it is gone.

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge position only to open and close the card. That reading is not saved or sent anywhere.

## Microphone

The app only asks for the microphone if you choose to record a voice message in the hidden setup. The recording is saved on your device and plays when the card opens. It is never uploaded or shared. You can delete it in setup, and deleting the app deletes it too. If you never record a message, the app never asks.

## Purchases

The app is a paid download, and Apple handles the whole payment through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
