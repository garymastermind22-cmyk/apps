# Pop-Up Valentine Card: Support

Something not working, or an idea for the card? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll reply.

## How does the card open?

On iPhone Duo, the hinge does it. Fold the phone and the front of the card shows on the cover display. Unfold it: the front swings away, the roses bloom along the arch, and once it is fully open the heart locket opens and the doves fly in. Fold and unfold again to see it all from the start. On any other iPhone, tap the front to open the card, and tap the small envelope in the bottom corner to close it.

## How do I add their name, my own message or my voice?

You don't have to: the card's own words work for anyone. To make it yours, hold the **top-left corner** of the card for one second, or triple-tap the open card. A hidden setup opens. There you can add their name (it shows on the front and as "Be Mine, ‹name›"), write your message, sign under "All my love", pick the music, pick the style and record a voice message of up to 30 seconds. **Preview the opening** plays the card through once. Tap **Done** to go back.

## Can I put the original words back?

Yes. In the hidden setup, tap **Restore the card's own words**. That clears the name and your signature and brings back the original message.

## How do I switch to the photograph?

Open the hidden setup (see above) and choose **Photograph** under **Style**. Choose **Illustrated** to go back to the paper-cut rose garden.

## Can I tap things in the card?

Yes. In the Illustrated card, tap a rose to make it bloom again, a lantern to make it glow, or a dove to make it flutter, and trail a finger through the falling petals. In the Photograph, tap the bougainvillea in the corner.

## There's no music

Turn the volume up. The card plays its music even when the phone is set to silent. The music starts once the card is fully open.

## Why does it want the microphone?

Only to record your voice message, and only after you tap Record in the hidden setup. The recording stays on your iPhone. If you never record, the app never asks.

## Do I need the internet?

No. Everything is built into the app, and it works with no connection at all.

## Why doesn't the screen go dark?

On purpose: the card should still be showing when you hand over the phone. Your usual auto-lock comes back as soon as you leave the app.
