# Little Joy Arcade: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll get back to you.

## How do I play?

Tap a machine in your arcade to play it. Slide the rail to move the claw, watch for the glowing ring, and press **GRAB**. When the ring glows, you'll catch the prize, and a caught prize is never dropped. Each win pays coins, and a new animal goes into your Sticker Book. Spend coins to level up a machine or build the next one. Customers play your machines too, so tap a coin box to collect what they paid.

## What does Little Joy Plus include?

Plus is a one-time purchase that opens the Arcade Row: a second, glowing room with five more games (Hay Loft, Bunny Bounce, Bubble Pond, Pup Parade and Seal Hoops), their 15 ribbons and an Arcade page in your Book. The Prize Hall, with its five claw machines and 30 animals, is complete and free. Plus never gives coins, levels or skips.

## Why does it ask me a maths question?

Before any purchase, and before resetting the game, the app shows a grown-up check: a multiplication written in words. It keeps younger players from buying or erasing anything by accident.

## How do I restore Little Joy Plus?

Open **Grown-ups** (answer the grown-up check) and tap **Restore**, or tap **Restore** on the Plus screen, while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is on for Plus, so family members can restore it the same way.

## Can I buy coins with real money?

No. Plus is the only purchase, and it's a one-time unlock. There are no ads, no coin packs and no paid speed-ups.

## I lost my progress

Your save lives on your device and is included in your iPhone, iPad or iCloud backup. Restoring the backup restores your progress. Deleting the app deletes the save.

## Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
