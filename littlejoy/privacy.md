# Little Joy Arcade: Privacy Policy

_Effective November 7, 2026_

Little Joy Arcade is a game made by Gary Guo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your device.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Little Joy Arcade does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no links out of the app, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites. The game doesn't use the internet.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your game on your device so you can pick up where you left off: your machines and their levels, coins, stickers, ribbons, settings, and whether you own Little Joy Plus. This data never leaves your device, except as part of your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge position only to arrange the screen layout. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Little Joy Plus is an optional one-time in-app purchase. It sits behind a grown-up check inside the app. Apple handles the payment entirely through the App Store, so we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only receives confirmation from the App Store that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Plus. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

## Children

Little Joy Arcade collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
