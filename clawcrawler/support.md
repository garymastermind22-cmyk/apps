# Claw Crawler: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[developer@garyguo.ca](mailto:developer@garyguo.ca)** and I'll get back to you.

## How do I play?

Steer the claw over the pile and press **Drop**. Everything that lands in the chute is yours: heroes join your party and items go on your shelf. Pick a quest, choose who goes and what they carry, and send them off. Quests run in real time, even while the app is closed. Come back to collect gold, stars and new prizes.

## What does Claw Crawler Plus include?

Plus is a one-time purchase that unlocks the whole game: every region after the first, including the final boss, plus Auto-Assign, extra crews so you can run several quests at once, Quick Claw and the top Royal Claw upgrades. The first region and its boss are free to play.

## How do I restore Claw Crawler Plus?

Open the **Plus** screen and tap **Restore Purchases**, while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. You can also find Restore Purchases in **Settings**. If Family Sharing is on for the purchase, family members can restore it the same way.

## Can I buy gold or speed up quests with real money?

No. Plus is the only purchase, and it's a one-time unlock. There are no ads, no consumables and no paid speed-ups.

## I lost my progress

Your save lives on your phone and is included in your iPhone or iCloud backup. Restoring the backup restores your progress. Deleting the app deletes the save.

## Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your phone. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
