# Grab Heroes: Privacy Policy

_Effective November 3, 2026_

Grab Heroes is a game made by Gary Guo. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Grab Heroes does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves your game on your device so you can pick up where you left off: your heroes and items, gold, quest progress and timers, unlocked regions, upgrades, collectibles, settings, and whether you own Grab Heroes Plus. This data never leaves your device, except as part of your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge position only to arrange the screen layout. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Grab Heroes Plus is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment entirely through the App Store, so we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only receives confirmation from the App Store that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Plus. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

## Children

Grab Heroes collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
